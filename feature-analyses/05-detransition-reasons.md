## Headline

- Detransition reasons are even more dispersed than transition reasons — **27.9%
  have no single dominant reason**.
- The named cluster is telling: **realisation-driven** exits (trauma, mental
  health, sex-based internalisation, ideology) are more common than
  **outcome-driven** exits (surgery regret, failure to pass).
- The largest single positive driver is remarkably constructive: **accepting
  one's sexed body** (529).

## Caveats

- "Ideological disillusionment" explicitly includes the medical system and
  community; it is not only political.
- These are retrospective narratives — people reinterpret early motives after
  detransitioning, and the model reads the account as written.
