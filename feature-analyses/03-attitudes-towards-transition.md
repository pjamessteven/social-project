# Attitude towards transition for others

*Choice distribution across all 5,030 accounts.*

## Distribution

| Attitude | Users | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | 1,707 | 33.9% |
| Opposed | 1,141 | 22.7% |
| Conditional | 828 | 16.5% |
| Not addressed | 794 | 15.8% |
| Supportive | 560 | 11.1% |

## Headline

- **A clear majority (56.6%) are critical or outright opposed** to transition
  for others, even though only ~34.6% regret their own transition.
- A substantial **conditional** group (16.5%) — people who would support
  transition under specific conditions (adult, thorough screening, no minors,
  therapy first, etc.).
- Roughly **1 in 9 remain supportive** of transition for others. The cohort is
  not uniformly anti-transition despite being a detransition community.

## Why it's interesting

This answer measures the *normative* stance people settled on after
detransitioning, not their medical history. It can be used to separate "detrans
but pro-transition" accounts (a meaningful minority) from the more vocal
critical/opposed majority — useful for interpreting the tone of individual
stories and for validating that the cohort is not monolithic.
