# Suicidality across the transition timeline

*Lifetime 849 (16.9%) · during transition 272 (5.4%) · since detransition 313 (6.2%).*

## Headline

This is one of the clearest timeline patterns in the dataset:

- **16.9%** of accounts describe suicidal ideation or attempts at some point.
- **5.4%** describe suicidality **during transition** (a question added last,
  run 5).
- **6.2%** describe suicidality **since detransitioning**.

The periods only partly overlap: 248 of the 272 "during transition" users also
flag lifetime ideation, but **only 45 also flag suicidality since detransition**.
Distress around transition and distress after it mostly concern different people.

## Patterns

- By sex: lifetime 16.6% female / 17.6% male; during transition 5.2% / 6.0%;
  since detransition 6.7% / 5.0%.
- By status (during transition): detransitioned 6.9%, still trans/questioning
  7.4%, desisters 2.9%, unclear 1.0%.
- Self-harm is separately reported by 521 accounts (10.4%).

## Caveats

- Suicidality is only measurable when an account mentions it; many users may not
  discuss it, so these are likely **minimum** figures.
- The three questions are asked independently, so overlap between periods is
  expected rather than contradictory.
