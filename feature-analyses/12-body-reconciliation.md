# Body reconciliation

*Feature tag "body reconciliation": 1,844 accounts (36.7%).*

## Headline

More than a third of the cohort describes **coming to accept their sexed body /
making peace with it** rather than continuing to want to change it — the most
common constructive outcome in the dataset after "detransition support".

## By status

| Status | Share with tag |
|---|---|
| Desisters | 48.4% |
| Detransitioned | 39.4% |
| Retransitioned | 14.5% |
| Still trans / questioning | 9.3% |
| Unclear | 1.6% |

## A surprising independence

Body reconciliation and transition regret are **largely independent dimensions**.
Among users who are regret-applicable, regret runs **46.5%** for those who score
body reconciliation ≥ 0.8 versus **44.5%** for those who don't — essentially no
difference.

In other words: accepting one's body does not mean believing transition was the
right decision, and regretting transition doesn't prevent making peace with the
body. The two axes should be reported separately rather than as one
"recovery" spectrum.

## Why it's interesting

Most detransition discourse focuses on negative outcomes (regret, complications).
Body reconciliation is the largest measurable *positive* endpoint and is
strongly skewed toward desisted users — people who often never went medical —
which may mean it is easiest to reach when the body was left more intact.
