## Headline

- **No single dominant reason exists for over a third of accounts** — these are
  genuinely mixed or unclear histories, not just a modelling artifact (Jev's
  confidence in "other/unclear" itself is often high).
- Where a reason is clear, the picture is **plural**: trauma, gender-role
  discomfort, sex-based internalisation, body dysmorphia and mental health each
  matter for large subgroups.
- **Sex differences are striking** (model argmax, share within each sex):
  - Females: internalised misogyny/misandry 12.2% vs males 4.5%.
  - Males: sexual factors (AGP/AAP) 12.2% vs females 1.7%.
  - Trauma is named more by females (14.5%) than males (9.2%).

## Caveats

- These are reasons the accounts themselves emphasise in a long history,
  compressed into one primary cause by the model; many users describe several.
- "Other/unclear" makes the pie chart honest but less quotable.
