## By status (anxiety)

Questioning 54.6%, still trans/questioning 54.6%, detransitioned 40.1%,
desisters 27.5%. Anxiety is highest among people still in transition
uncertainty.

## Model scores

- Mental-health severity: mean **1.59 / 3**.
- Mental-health change after detransition: mean **2.70 / 4** (positive direction).
- Overall quality of life: mean **2.69 / 4**.

## Caveats

- "Any condition" answers reflect what users disclose; people discuss anxiety and
  depression far more readily than BPD or psychosis, so the tail is undercounted.
- High general prevalence means individual conditions should be read as relative
  signals, not diagnoses.
