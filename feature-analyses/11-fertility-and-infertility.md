# Fertility and infertility

*Feature tags: now infertile 216 (4.3%) · fertility preservation desire 178
(3.5%) · infertility regret 92 (1.8%).*

## Headline

- **216 accounts state they are now infertile** — a small but consequential
  share, and almost certainly an undercount: many users never discuss fertility.
- 178 accounts express desire to preserve fertility; 92 explicitly regret
  infertility.
- Infertility regret is also part of the broader **hormone and body regret** tag
  (881), and 280 accounts carry **top surgery regret** and 75 **bottom surgery
  regret**.

## Why it's interesting

Fertility is one of the few consequences that is *not* re-interpretable after
detransition: unlike social loss or identity confusion, infertility is physical
and permanent. The ratio matters for practice — roughly **1.8% of the entire
cohort explicitly regrets infertility**, among a group where half took hormones.
The gap between "fertility preservation desire" (178) and "now infertile" (216)
suggests preservation was often discussed but not always acted on, or not
available at the time.

## Caveats

- These are self-reports; no medical verification exists.
- Fertility regret likely clusters with younger transitioners and longer
  hormone exposure, but age data is too noisy in the timelines to quantify here.
