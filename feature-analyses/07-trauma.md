# Trauma

*Feature tag "trauma history": 1,857 accounts (36.9%).*

## Headline

Trauma appears both as **background** and as **driver**, and the two are very
different numbers:

- **History** (any trauma/abuse relevant to the story): **36.9%** of accounts.
- **Primary transition reason** named as trauma: **655 (13.0%)**.
- **Primary detransition reason** named as trauma realisation: **549 (10.9%)**.

So roughly one in three accounts carries trauma, but only about one in eight
believes it caused the transition — and a similar-sized group only recognised
the link *when detransitioning*.

(47.3%) accounts, lower among desisters (30.5%) and questioners (18.5%).

## Why it's interesting

Trauma is the single most common named transition reason (13.0%) after
"other/unclear", yet it is not reducible to a single event type: bullying,
childhood abuse and sexual assault each capture different populations. The
"realisation" framing (recognising the trauma link only in hindsight) is a
distinct and common arc in detransition stories.
