# Regret of transition

*1,740 of 5,030 accounts (34.6%) tagged "regrets transitioning" (p ≥ 0.8).*

## Headline

- Regret is **bimodal**: most accounts are decided one way or the other.
  1,303 accounts score p ≥ 0.9, while 1,762 sit below p = 0.2.
- A further **1,190 accounts (23.7%)** are scored *regret not applicable* — mostly
  desisters, questioners and observers who never had a medical transition to
  regret. Any regret statistic should be read net of this group.
- Among users who currently describe themselves as **detransitioned** (stopped
  after a medical step): **54.2% regret** (1,474 / 2,719). Among **desisters**
  (no medical intervention): **26.9%** (171 / 635) — markedly lower, but not
  negligible.

## Trajectory

Among the 1,740 regretters, Jev's trajectory answer splits: **improving 40%**,
mixed 28%, worsening 13%, resolved 10%, stable 9%. In other words, regret is
more often moving toward resolution than deepening.

## Caveats

- "Not applicable" is a probability of its own (1,190 at p ≥ 0.8); small numbers
  of intense-regret cases can still appear there, which is why the tag sync can
  gate intensity-derived tags behind `regret_not_applicable < 0.8`.
- Explicit "doesn't regret transitioning" is rare (232, 4.6%) and concentrates
  among still-transitioning and desisted accounts.
