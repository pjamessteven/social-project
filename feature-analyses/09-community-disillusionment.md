# Community disillusionment

*Feature tag: 2,414 accounts (48.0%).*

## Headline

Nearly half the cohort expresses disillusionment with the trans/LGBT community
at some point — one of the most prevalent features in the dataset.

- **81.1% of tagged accounts actually transitioned** (they are not merely
  external critics): 1,957 of 2,414 score neither "no detransition occurred" nor
  "never transitioned".
- A separate, narrower tag — **online community disillusionment** — applies to
  927 accounts (18.4%). The gap captures people whose disillusionment is with
  the wider movement rather than online spaces specifically.
- Only 234 accounts score "ideological involvement", so this is mostly an
  after-the-fact reaction, not a pre-existing political identity.

## Why it's interesting

Community disillusionment is *not* the same as ideological disillusionment as a
detransition reason (8.1%): many people can criticise the community while still
endorsing their own transition or the concept generally. Separating the two is
one of the clearer wins of the new taxonomy.
