# Pathways: from first dysphoria to outcome

*How accounts move through the transition journey — entry, escalation, exit.*

## The shape of the journey

- **First dysphoria → transition**: median **6 years** (n = 412 with usable
  ages; quartiles 2–12).
- **Transition → detransition**: median **4 years** (n = 1,033; quartiles 2–6).
  About 45% stop within 3 years; 7% stay 11+ years.

Both figures come from model-extracted ages/years and exist only for a subset of
accounts, but they give the journey a realistic scale: typically years, not
months.

## The medical escalation funnel

| Tier | Users | Share | Regret* | Body reconciliation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Never transitioned | 704 | 14.0% | — | 30.8% |
| Social / no medical | 1,701 | 33.8% | 21.5% | 37.1% |
| Hormones (or blockers), no surgery | 1,770 | 35.2% | 49.3% | 37.2% |
| Any surgery | 855 | 17.0% | **70.9%** | 39.4% |

*Regret among regret-applicable users (not scored "not applicable").

**Two mirror-image findings:**

1. **Regret rises monotonically with medical depth** — 21% → 49% → 71%. The
   further people went, the more likely they regret it.
2. **Body reconciliation stays flat (~31–39%)** across every tier. Making peace
   with the body is not the inverse of regretting the decision, and it is
   achievable whether or not the body was altered.

## Where people exit

| Current status | Never | Social only | Medical (no surgery) | Surgery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desisters (1,497) | 38.2% | 61.7% | 0.1% | 0% |
| Detransitioned (2,734) | 0.1% | 16.8% | 55.0% | 28.1% |
| Still trans / questioning (324) | 2.5% | 17.0% | 59.0% | 21.6% |
| Retransitioned (55) | 0% | 9.1% | 74.5% | 16.4% |

The entry point largely determines the exit: **desisters are by definition
non-medical** — 1,495 of 1,497 carry no medical tag, and the two flagged cases
are false positives (a cancer mastectomy scored as "top surgery", and a "took
hormones" answer the account itself contradicts). Detransitioned users are 83%
medical or surgical; retransitioners 91%.

## Minors vs adults

Among the 1,288 accounts with a usable transition age (10–40) — a subset
enriched for detransitioned users, so read relatively:

| | Under 18 (713) | 18+ (575) |
|---|---|---|
| Puberty blockers | 12.1% | 1.4% |
| Hormones | 76.4% | 90.1% |
| Top surgery | 27.2% | 27.3% |
| Social only | 17.1% | 6.3% |

Minors were **far more likely to have taken blockers and to have transitioned
socially only**, while adults were more likely to have used hormones. Top
surgery rates were nearly identical across the two groups.

## Caveats

- Tier assignments use p ≥ 0.8 tags and reflect disclosure, not records.
- Age/year subsections are not representative (they over-represent
  detransitioned, highly engaged accounts) and contain parse noise.
- "Regret" here is the account's stated regret, not a clinical assessment.
